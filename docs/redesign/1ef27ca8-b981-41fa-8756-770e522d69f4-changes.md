# Session 1ef27ca8: finished session, goal and outcome written, then analyzed

**Path:** 1. A session that has already finished needs a goal and an expected outcome, then an
analysis.

**Session:** Haiku 5.5 implementing the analysis-reason guard in `compass` (worktree
`haiku-4809`). It finished its turn and opened a draft PR before I arrived. Observed on the
`:4560` build of `feature/session-redesign`.

**References:** the Session Redesign design (`Main.dc.html`) and the sample session.

Severity: **P1** breaks the flow or misleads, **P2** departs visibly from the design, **P3** polish.

## Arriving at a finished session

1. **P2 · "Finished" reads as `idle · awaiting your message`.** The agent had finished the task and
   printed its PR, but the pill says `idle`. Compass cannot know the work is done, but the page could
   say "Turn ended 11:00 · waiting for you" in the design's one-line header instead.
2. **P1 · An idle session shows a live token rate.** The header still read `4,106 tokens/min`
   minutes after the last turn ended. A rate on a session that is doing nothing reads as activity.
3. **P2 · The project is the worktree folder.** The header link and the Sessions list show
   `worktrees/haiku-4809`, not `compass`. Every session started in an isolated worktree is filed
   under a project named after the worktree, so a repository's sessions scatter across one project
   per branch.
4. **P2 · Three entry counts for one session.** At the same moment the header said `13 entries`
   (then `20 entries` after a refresh), the summary estimate said `About 22 entries to read`, and the
   outline said `Entries #1 to #13`. A reader cannot tell which is the session's size.
5. **P2 · One outline phase for the whole session.** The finished session is one phase, headed with
   the whole first prompt: `13 messages with 9 checks and files under them · 12 agent replies · 4
   checks run · 5 files written in frontend/src/drift (5)`. The `(5)` repeats the 5. The design's
   card is a short paragraph and 3 to 5 bullets saying what was done.
6. **P2 · Empty Drift card controls.** The same as the running case: `Pause model calls`, `Live
   monitor` and its sentence, `Analyze drift`, `0 drift checks requested`. None of these are in the
   design.

## Writing and saving the intent

7. **P3 · `Unsaved changes` turns amber.** It is the only amber text in the Intent card and it sits
   next to `How Intent and drift works`. That is the right signal, but in the design the status word
   alone (`Saved`) is enough, with `Undo changes` beside `Save intent`.
8. **P2 · The "Use your prompt" select changes label after typing.** It reads `Use your prompt`
   before typing and `Replace with your prompt` after. A select whose label is an action, and which
   changes, is easy to trigger by mistake. The design's button opens a list of the session's prompts.
9. **P3 · `+ Add a line` is found by text, not by place.** It moves down with each new line, sits
   alone on the left, and `Save intent` is on the far right. With three lines the two controls are
   about 900px apart.

## The analysis of a finished session

10. **P1 · The level says Medium and the verdict says it can't tell.** The card reads `Can't tell
    from what was read.` in bold, then `Medium` with the bar filled to Medium, and the page header
    pill reads `Drift: Medium`. All four lines are `Can't tell`, and there are no departures. A level
    with no departure behind it is the "structurally-present default" the repository's invariants
    warn about: Medium is reported where nothing was found.
11. **P1 · Steer back is offered with nothing to steer from.** The card shows `Steer back · from the
    check at 11:03` with no departure on the reading. The correction it builds has nothing to say.
12. **P2 · Every line says the same thing twice.** Each line is `Can't tell: what was read does not
    settle this · "<line>"`, and a bold `Can't tell` heading sits above the list. The design's line
    is `Can't tell · <line>` followed by a reason specific to that line.
13. **P2 · Evidence is a list of entry kinds.** Under each line: `File write [8.1]`, `File write
    [8.2]`, `passed check [10.1]`, `not-recorded check [11.3]`. The reader learns the kinds of
    entries, not what they show. `not-recorded check` has no reason (the open issue about saying why
    a check has no recorded result).
14. **P1 · The PR line is `Can't tell` though the record has the PR.** "A draft PR is open with the
    change and its tests." The agent ran `gh pr create --draft` and printed PR #632. The reading
    cites only agent messages `[12]` and `[13]`. If the reading cannot see the command's output, the
    line should say that, not "what was read does not settle this".
15. **P2 · "What the agent said at [13] is not shown by the record".** It is the same unexplained
    row as in the running case, with no quote of what was claimed.
16. **P2 · No way to read again without a later direction.** After the result, the card offers
    `Steer back` and `Mark as inaccurate`, but no `Analyze again`. On a finished session with no new
    work and no later direction, a reader who edits nothing has no re-run control in view. When the
    intent is edited, the pre-analysis `Analyze drift` comes back.
17. **P2 · `Claim to check at [13] · 4m ago`.** It sits under the Steer back row with no
    explanation. It reads like an instruction to the reader.
18. **P3 · `What is sent ⌄` is now under the counter.** It moves between three places in the card
    depending on state (under `Analyze drift`, inside the amber "new work" box, under `1 drift check
    requested`).

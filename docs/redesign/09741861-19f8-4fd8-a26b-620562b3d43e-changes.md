# Session 09741861: updating the intent

**Path:** 3. A session whose intent needs updating.

**Session:** Haiku 5.5 giving each mounted ProjectTabs instance its own ids in `compass` (worktree
`haiku-4852`, draft PR #633). I saved an intent, then sent the agent a direction that widens the
scope ("also make the focus-lane keys per-instance"). After it finished, I used `Add it to my
intent`, analyzed, then rewrote the goal to cover the new scope and checked again. Observed on the
`:4560` build of `feature/session-redesign`.

**References:** the Session Redesign design (`Main.dc.html`) and the sample session.

Severity: **P1** breaks the flow or misleads, **P2** departs visibly from the design, **P3** polish.

## The prompt picker

1. **P3 · The picker opens on "Loading your prompts".** `Use your prompt` opens a menu headed
   "Your prompts in this session" that says `Loading your prompts` for about a second, then lists
   `First prompt · 10:57 · excerpt`. That is close to the design's list. The trigger is still styled
   as a native select.

## A later direction arrives before any analysis

2. **P1 · `Analyze drift` is replaced by three direction buttons.** With an intent saved and one
   later message from me, the Drift card showed `Not checked yet`, an empty bar, `1 later
   direction.` and `Keep my intent and analyze` / `Add it to my intent` / `Use this as my goal`.
   There was no plain `Analyze drift`. The direction itself was not quoted (the same as e14f8b08
   item 29). The reader must decide what to do with a message they cannot see before they can get
   any reading at all.
3. **P2 · The decision is in the Drift card, but it changes the Intent card.** Two of the three
   buttons edit the intent, which is a card above. The design has no later-direction surface. The
   natural place to settle a direction is next to the intent, as a quoted suggestion under the
   expected outcome ("Your 11:05 message adds: … · Add as a line · Make it the goal · Ignore").

## Add it to my intent

4. **P2 · It pastes the whole message as a draft line.** `Add it to my intent` inserted a two-line
   field holding my message verbatim, including "run the targeted tests, and push to the same
   branch and PR", with `201 / 240 from #7 · not saved`, its own small `Save`, an `×`, and the hint
   "Write the rule, not the moment." The hint is good advice, but the field starts as the moment.
   Offer a trimmed rule as the starting text, or put the hint in the placeholder of an empty line.
5. **P2 · Two save buttons at once.** While that draft line was open, the card had the line's own
   `Save` and the card's `Save intent`, and the status at the top still said `Saved`. It is not clear
   whether `Save intent` saves the draft line too.
6. **P3 · After saving, the line becomes outcome 2 with `added from #7`.** That is clear. The `#7`
   is an entry number the reader has not seen unless they opened Full activity.

## Analysis after adding the direction

7. **P1 · Every line is consistent and the level is "Not enough recorded yet".** The reading said
   `Nothing found against what it read.` and each of the three lines was `Consistent with what the
   agent said at [6]` (or `[11]`). The bar is empty, labelled `Not enough recorded yet`, with `Why
   not None or low ⌄`. When every line is consistent, the reader expects None or low. If the level
   is withheld because consistency rests only on the agent's own messages, the card has to say that
   in one line instead of behind a "why not" disclosure.
8. **P2 · "Consistent with what the agent said" is the only evidence.** Each line rests on an agent
   message alone. The outline shows 6 and 4 checks run, but none are cited. The design separates
   "What the agent claimed" from what the record shows. Here, a claim reads as support.
9. **P3 · The outline lists checks but no files.** The phases say `6 checks run` and `4 checks
   run`, with no "files written", although the agent edited `ProjectTabs.tsx` and its tests. Either
   the edits were not recognised as writes, or the phase line omits them.

## Rewriting the goal

10. **P1 · Saving a new goal silently moves the reading window.** Before: `Saved · reads from
    10:57`. After rewriting the goal: `Saved · reads from 11:05`, and the header added `15 entries, 8
    since your intent's start`. On `Check again`, the reading only read entries 7 to 11. The line "A
    test mounts two instances and finds every id unique." went from `Consistent` to `Can't tell`,
    because the test was written in entries 1 to 6, which are now outside the window. Editing a goal to
    *widen* scope should not drop the work already done. If moving the window is intended, say so in
    the same moment ("Compass now reads from 11:05, when you changed the goal. Earlier work is not
    read") and offer to keep the original start.
11. **P2 · "This reading read revision 2. Revision 3 is current".** The stale-reading banner says
    `Your intent changed after this analysis.` and then names revision numbers. "Revision" appears
    nowhere else on the page. "This reading is of your 11:05 intent; you changed it at 11:09" says
    the same with times the reader has seen.
12. **P2 · The stale banner repeats the amber box pattern.** `Check again`, `1 drift check
    requested` and `What is sent ⌄` sit inside it again, between the verdict heading and the lines.
    See e14f8b08 item 42.
13. **P3 · `Saved · reads from 11:05 ⌄`.** This is the most consequential line in the Intent card,
    because it decides what is read, and it is the smallest text in the meta row.

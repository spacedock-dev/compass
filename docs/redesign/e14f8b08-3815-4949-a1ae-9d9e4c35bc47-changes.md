# Session e14f8b08: running session, goal and outcome written, then analyzed

**Path:** 2. A running session that needs a goal and an expected outcome, then an analysis.

**Session:** Haiku 5.5 working on the CLL missing-parent issue in `recce` (worktree
`recce-haiku-4918`), observed live on the `:4560` build of `feature/session-redesign`.

**References:** the Session Redesign design (`Main.dc.html` in the design artifact) and the sample
session (`#n=session:acme%2Fdashboard:claude:sample`).

Severity: **P1** breaks the flow or misleads, **P2** departs visibly from the design, **P3** polish.

## Arriving: a running session with no intent

1. **P2 · Section order.** The page goes Intent, Drift, What the session has done. The design goes
   Intent, What the session has done, Drift. With no analysis yet, the Drift card sits between the
   goal and the work and holds nothing but controls.
2. **P2 · Header.** The design has one quiet line under the title: `claude · Working · running Bash ·
   turn 11m`. The build has two lines: `claude · e14f8b08 · 1,450 tokens/min · recce/... · Started
   about 57s ago`, then a `working` pill and `Claude · running Bash · turn started 54s ago · 7
   entries`. "claude" and "Claude" both appear, and the entry count and token rate change on every
   refresh.
3. **P2 · Intent status.** The design shows one word on the right of the card (`Saved`). The build
   shows `Draft from your first prompt, not saved` plus a `How Intent and drift works` disclosure.
   The disclosure is not in the design.
4. **P2 · "Use your prompt".** The design has a button that opens a list, "Your prompts in this
   session". The build has a native-looking `<select>` that says `Use your prompt` before saving and
   `Replace with your prompt` after.
5. **P2 · Goal field.** The design uses a three-row textarea (a goal can be 240 characters). The
   build uses a one-line input, so a long goal scrolls sideways out of sight.
6. **P3 · Goal hint.** `From your prompt · first · not saved. Analyze reads at most the first 1,000
   characters of any prompt.` is long, and its second sentence is about prompts rather than the
   goal. The design reads `Typed · saved 14:16`.
7. **P2 · Empty expected outcome.** With no lines, the first row has no × button and there is no
   `+ Add a line` until the reader types. In the design, `+ Add a line` and `Save intent` always sit
   together on the left under the lines.
8. **P2 · Button placement.** The build puts `Save intent` (and later `Undo changes`) on the right
   and `+ Add a line` on the left. The design keeps `+ Add a line`, `Save intent` and `Undo changes`
   together, left-aligned.
9. **P2 · Clear row.** Before anything is saved, a `Clear` button and "Drift is measured against
   these. Edit anything that is off." sit under the form. The design has neither. `Clear` beside an
   empty form reads as a destructive control with nothing to clear.
10. **P2 · Empty Drift card.** It shows `Pause model calls`, a `Live monitor` switch and its
    sentence, `Analyze drift`, `0 drift checks requested`, and `What is sent to Claude Code`. The
    design's Drift card has none of the controls, only a heading, the model and read time on the
    right, and the result. "0 drift checks requested" counts something that has not happened, and a
    screen reader hears it twice (it is in the page text twice).
11. **P3 · Repeated disclosure.** `What is sent to Claude Code` appears in both the Drift card and
    the What the session has done card.

## What the session has done, before a summary

12. **P2 · Intro copy.** Before the first summary, the card opens with three sentences about what
    a summary is, then `About 12 entries to read in 2 parts, up to 10 minutes.`, then `Summarize`.
    The design shows the session's story, not an explanation of one. The estimate is useful, but it
    should sit beside the button rather than above it.
13. **P2 · Outline text.** Each outline phase is dense mono text (`8 messages with 4 checks and
    files under them · 7 agent replies · 3 checks run, 1 failed as the tool reported · 1 file
    written in tests/adapter/dbt_adapter (1)`). The design's bullets are short sentences.
14. **P1 · A planned red test reads as a failure.** The first phase shows, in red, `Failed as the
    tool reported: python -m pytest ...`. That run is the agent writing a failing test first, which
    is what the issue asked for. In red, at the top of a fresh session, it reads as something gone
    wrong.
15. **P3 · Phase anchors.** `Starts at #1` and `Entries #1 to #8.2` are reference numbers with no
    meaning to a reader who has not opened Full activity.

## Writing and saving the intent

16. **P1 · The form briefly shows the draft again while saving.** After `Save intent` was
    pressed, the button showed `Saving…`, and for about two seconds the goal reverted to the drafted
    prompt and the expected outcome went empty. The typed words came back once the save finished. A
    reader watching it thinks the save threw their words away.
17. **P2 · Analyze is disabled while edits are unsaved, but the reason is in another card.** The
    hatched `Analyze drift` and "Save your intent, or undo your edit, to analyze drift." sit in the
    Drift card, well below the `Save intent` button the reader needs to press.
18. **P2 · After saving, five meta controls appear.** `Saved · reads from 10:57 ⌄`, `Clear`, the
    "Drift is measured…" sentence, `About these saved words ⌄`, `Discard everything ⌄` and
    `Later directions: none ⌄` fill a row under the form. The design has none of them, and only
    "Saved as a new revision." is needed in that moment.
19. **P3 · Save stays primary after saving.** `Save intent` keeps its accent outline when nothing
    has changed. In the design it is the action you take, not a resting state.
20. **P2 · Drift before analysis.** After saving, the Drift card shows `Not checked yet`, an empty
    four-step level bar, `Analyze drift` and `Steer back`. `Steer back` is offered before there is a
    reading to steer from, and the empty level bar shows four labels for no data.

## Pressing Analyze drift on the running session

21. **P2 · The first click missed.** The page scrolled under the pointer between frames: an outline
    phase finished arriving and pushed the Drift card down, so the first press of `Analyze drift`
    landed on empty space. Live content above the reader's target should not move it. Either keep
    the cards above in place while the reader is in Drift, or keep the outline below the controls.
22. **P2 · Running state stacks on top of "Not checked yet".** While the analysis ran, the card
    still said `Not checked yet` over an empty four-step bar, with the progress list (`Preparing
    what is sent`, `Waiting for Claude Code`, `Checking the reply`) and `Cancel` underneath, and
    `0 drift checks requested`. The design has no running state. The heading should switch to
    "Analyzing…" and replace the empty bar instead of sitting beside it.
23. **P1 · The one-time notice is a wall in the middle of the result.** The first analysis opened
    "This request sends:" inline: seven bullets, `Provider / Words go to / Tool output goes to`, a
    second `Pause model calls` button and its sentence. It stays open after the result arrives, so
    the result is pushed below about 400px of disclosure. A one-time notice should be dismissible
    and collapse to `What is sent` once read. Neither the design nor the sample shows it.
24. **P1 · One result said three times.** The result shows `So far: Can't tell from what was read.`,
    then `Not enough recorded yet` over an empty bar, then lower down `So far: Can't tell`. The
    design states the verdict once (`Departs from your intent · 2 departures`).
25. **P2 · `Why not None or low ⌄`.** It is offered when there is no level at all. "Why not the
    lowest level" makes no sense when the level is "not enough recorded".
26. **P2 · "Coverage was not recorded for this reading."** A sentence about missing internal data,
    shown to every reader. It does not say what the reader can do.
27. **P2 · Per-line verdicts have no reason.** Each line is `Can't tell · "<line>"` with an
    `Evidence ⌄` disclosure. The design's line reads `Can't tell · <line>` followed by why ("A check
    on record failed after your intent, so the evidence can't confirm this either way."). Here the
    reader has to open each disclosure to learn why.
28. **P2 · Analysis ran on 13 entries and could tell nothing.** By the time the reading returned,
    the agent had written the fix in `recce/adapter/dbt_adapter` and the test in
    `tests/adapter/dbt_adapter`. Both lines were still `Can't tell`. Early in a running session the
    card should say so ("The session is 3 minutes in; read again when it has run its checks") rather
    than offering an empty verdict.
29. **P2 · Later direction with no quote.** Under the result: `1 later direction since saving your
    intent.` and three equal-weight buttons: `Keep my intent and analyze`, `Add it to my intent`,
    `Use this as my goal`. The Drift card does not show what the direction says, so the reader has
    to choose blind. The direction is only visible as the second outline phase further down the
    page. Quote it, and make one choice primary.
30. **P2 · "This check is from before your 11:00 message."** It is in an outlined box below the
    disclosure, far from the verdict it qualifies. It should sit beside the verdict's timestamp in
    the card header, the way the design has `claude-sonnet-5-5 · read at 14:27`.
31. **P3 · `Analyze drift` is gone after a result.** The only path to read again is `Keep my intent
    and analyze`, which only exists because a later direction arrived. With no direction there would
    be no re-run control in view (to verify on a finished session).
32. **P3 · Mark as inaccurate takes a full row.** `Mark as inaccurate` plus a two-line explanation
    sits beside `What the analysis read ⌄`. The design does not show it. It belongs in a menu or a
    quiet footer.
33. **P3 · Where the work went.** Folder paths with `[8.1]` citations sit under the line verdicts
    with no explanation. The design does not include this block in the Drift card.

## Answering the agent, then "Keep my intent and analyze"

The agent asked a product question (whether to attribute unresolved columns to the uncataloged
parent). I answered in the session: keep option 1, but fix the same pattern in `_get_schema` with a
test, and push.

35. **P1 · The first press of `Keep my intent and analyze` only reveals the direction.** It did not
    start an analysis. It showed `#14 Keep option 1 only; ...` above the buttons and the hint `Read
    each direction, then press again`. The reader is made to press twice because the direction was
    hidden the first time. Show the quoted direction before the first press (see 29) and let the
    first press act.
36. **P2 · The previous result vanishes while the next one runs.** During the re-run, the card
    showed `Not enough recorded yet`, the progress list and `1 drift check requested`. The earlier
    verdict lines were gone. Keep the last result in view, dimmed, with "Reading again…" until the
    new one lands.
37. **P1 · A running session is read as if it had ended.** The second reading returned `Medium so
    far: 1 departure`. Its "why" says "the session ends with the probe still showing wrong output, no
    rerun and no push". The session had not ended: the agent was mid-turn, running the very rerun
    the reading says is missing. Unfinished work in a running session is reported as a departure.
    Until the turn stops, the reading has to treat "not done yet" as in progress, not as departing.
38. **P1 · The departure is pinned to the reader's own direction.** The departure's `What` leads
    with `You said: "Keep option 1 only; ..." [14]`. That direction was then kept with `Keep my
    intent and analyze`. The reader is told they caused the drift.
39. **P2 · Departure title is the goal, not what happened.** The card's bold title repeats the
    whole goal sentence. The design titles each departure with what happened ("It changed PR 56's
    code"), then `What` as one sentence, then `Why Compass reads this as drift`, then `Departs;
    evidence #16, #17`.
40. **P2 · `What` is a list of raw quotes.** `You said: "…" [14]`, `Agent said: "…" [22]`, and the
    second quote is cut mid-word ("…and noth"). The design's `What` is a plain sentence written for
    the reader.
41. **P2 · `So far:` is repeated and dangling.** The card reads `Medium so far: 1 departure, at your
    message at 11:00 and the agent's message at 11:01.`, then `Medium` over the bar, then `So far:
    Departs from your intent · 1 departure`. In the plain-text read the label `So far:` stands on a
    line of its own.
42. **P2 · "New work since this analysis" is an amber box between the level and the result.** It
    holds `Check again`, `2 drift checks requested` and `What is sent ⌄`. It splits the level from
    the departures it explains. In the design, the read time sits in the card header and there is no
    box. "New work since 11:02 · Check again" fits in that header line.
43. **P2 · "What the agent said at [13] is not shown by the record".** The row is `What the agent
    said at [13] is not shown by the record` / `Agent message [13]` / `Evidence ⌄`. It never says
    what the agent claimed. The design's row reads `What the agent claimed · "All green locally: 473
    tests pass" is not shown by the record`, with a reason and a single citation.
44. **P2 · `Update intent instead` and `Steer back` sit below "Where the work went".** In the design,
    Steer back is its own section after the departures, with a heading, one sentence and the
    correction to paste. Here both are inline buttons, and `Update intent instead` uses the dashed
    "add" style that `+ Add a line` uses.
45. **P3 · The header pill changes to `Drift: Medium`.** It does not say the level is provisional
    while the session runs, unlike the card ("so far").
